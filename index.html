Our Story

Decades of Trust & Expertise

SOMET Group was established in 1987 by its present CEO and owner, Yadin Segal. With a vision to create a premier truck service facility, the Somet Truck Center was built at Port Elizabeth, New Jersey — strategically positioned at the heart of the region's freight corridors.

At Somet Truck Center, we pride ourselves on our many years of experience. Our technicians have had extensive training in all types of repairs. We carry a full inventory of truck parts and tires to meet our customers' needs. Our five-acre plus facility allows for easy access and mobility.
