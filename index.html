Maintenance

Preventive Maintenance That Protects Uptime

Keeping your truck properly maintained is the key to reducing downtime, avoiding costly repairs, and staying road-ready year-round.

Daily / Pre-Trip

Before every trip, drivers should check fluids, tires, lights, brakes, and any visible leaks or issues to help prevent breakdowns on the road.

Routine Service Intervals

At 5,000 to 15,000 mile intervals, regular service includes lubrication, brake inspections, tire evaluations, and oil and filter maintenance based on operating conditions.

Major Service Intervals

At higher mileage intervals, deeper inspections and service are performed on transmissions, differentials, cooling systems, and emissions components for long-term durability.

Annual DOT & Safety Inspections

We perform full DOT inspections and comprehensive safety evaluations to keep your truck compliant, legal, and operating safely on the road.

Built for Uptime

Our preventive maintenance approach is designed to catch issues early, reduce unexpected breakdowns, and keep your fleet moving with minimal downtime.

We follow industry-standard maintenance schedules designed to keep heavy-duty equipment operating safely and efficiently at every stage of its life cycle.
